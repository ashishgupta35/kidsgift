KidsGift.in is a participant in the **Amazon Associates Program**, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.in (and affiliated sites).

**What this means in practice**

- Some links on this site — including "View on Amazon" buttons and links inside blog posts — are affiliate links.
- If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission. This comes at **no extra cost to you**; the price you pay is the same either way.
- We only link to products we've genuinely chosen to recommend. Being part of an affiliate program doesn't change which products make it onto a list — it only affects how a purchase, once you've decided to make it, gets tracked.

**As required by Amazon**

As an Amazon Associate, KidsGift.in earns from qualifying purchases made through links to Amazon.in.

**Other programs**

If we join additional affiliate programs beyond Amazon Associates in the future, this page will be updated to reflect that.
