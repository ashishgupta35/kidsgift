**What we collect**

This site is a static site — there's no login system, no user accounts, and no database storing personal information. If you use the contact form, your message is sent directly to our email via your own email client; we don't store it on any server we run.

**Cookies and analytics**

If we add an analytics tool (for example, Google Analytics) or display ads in the future, that service may set cookies or collect anonymised usage data (pages visited, approximate location, device type) to help us understand traffic. This page will be updated to name the specific tool(s) in use once added.

**Affiliate links**

Links to Amazon and other retailers may track that a visit originated from this site, for the purpose of attributing any resulting purchase to our affiliate account. See our [Affiliate Disclosure]({{ '/affiliate-disclosure/' | relative_url }}) for details.

**Third-party sites**

Once you click through to Amazon or any other retailer, their own privacy policy applies — we have no control over their data practices.

**Contact**

Questions about this policy can be sent via our [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).
