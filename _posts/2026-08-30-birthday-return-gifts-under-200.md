Return gifts have one job: not get binned before the guest reaches home. That rules out most of the cheap plastic novelty items that dominate this price bracket — they tend to break within the hour.

## What holds up at scale

A small storybook or colouring set costs barely more than a plastic toy but has a much longer shelf life at home — parents are more likely to let it stick around because it doesn't look or feel like junk. Buying in packs also brings the per-unit price down further than buying single items one at a time.

For a mixed-age guest list, small puzzle cubes are a safe middle ground — durable, screen-free, and something a 4-year-old and an 8-year-old can both get some use out of.
