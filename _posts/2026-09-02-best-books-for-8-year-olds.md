"Doesn't like reading" is often really "hasn't found the right book yet." Eight is an age where kids are reading independently but still want something that moves fast — long chapters without payoff lose them quickly.

## What tends to work

Graphic novels get an undeserved reputation as "not real reading." In practice, they're one of the most reliable ways to hook a reluctant 8-year-old, because the visual pacing keeps them moving forward instead of stalling on a dense paragraph.

For kids who are more curious than narrative-driven, a well-illustrated fact book or encyclopedia works better than fiction — they can dip in and out without needing to follow a plot, and it rewards them for reading in short bursts.
