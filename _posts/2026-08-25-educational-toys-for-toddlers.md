At this age, the toys that stick around aren't the ones labelled "educational" the loudest — they're the ones that happen to teach something while being genuinely fun to handle: stacking, sorting, matching colours, turning pages.

## What actually works at 1-3 years

Chunky, easy-to-grip pieces beat anything fiddly. A toddler's motor skills are still developing, so a puzzle with three or four large pieces gets far more use than a 12-piece set that ends up frustrating instead of fun.

Shape sorters remain one of the most durable picks in this category — they're simple enough for a one-year-old to bang around and complex enough that a two-and-a-half-year-old is still working out the harder shapes.

Soft fabric books deserve more credit than they get. They survive drool, chewing, and being thrown across a room — a real durability advantage over board books at this stage.
