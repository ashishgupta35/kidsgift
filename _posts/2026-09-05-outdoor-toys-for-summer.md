Outdoor toys take a beating — sun, dust, being dropped on concrete — so durability matters more here than almost anywhere else on this site. A few of the usual suspects hold up better than most.

## What lasts

A sand and water play table earns its higher price by covering a wider age range and staying interesting across an entire summer, not just a single hot week. Look for one with a drainage plug — it makes cleanup and storage far less of a chore.

Simple catch games — badminton, frisbee — remain underrated. They're light, cheap to replace individual pieces for, and get kids moving without needing a whole afternoon set aside.
