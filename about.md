KidsGift.in is a small, independently-run guide to gifts for kids — birthdays, festivals, "just because," all of it.

We started it because most gift-guide sites online are really just re-shuffled product lists with no real opinion behind them. We try to do the opposite: every pick gets one honest question — *would we actually give this to a child we know?* If the answer's no, it doesn't make the list, no matter how well it might sell.

**How we pick products**

- We prioritise safety, durability, and age-appropriateness over trending or "viral" items.
- We note real downsides, not just selling points.
- Prices and availability are checked periodically, but always confirm on the retailer's page before buying.

**How this site makes money**

Some links on this site are affiliate links — mainly through the Amazon Associates program. If you buy something after clicking one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It doesn't change which price you pay, and it doesn't influence which products we choose to recommend.
